What a peer is. A peer is another news server this node exchanges articles with. Only peers may feed us with IHAVE, MODE STREAM, CHECK and TAKETHIS; NNTP users can no longer feed, whatever their posting flag says (posting now means POST and nothing else). A disabled peer may neither feed us nor be fed.

Hosts. A comma separated list of IP addresses (192.0.2.7, 2001:db8::1), CIDR ranges (192.0.2.0/24) and host names (news.example.org, resolved and cached). A connection from a matching address is that peer's connection. Leave it empty to match the peer by its AUTHINFO login only. With both set, the peer matches by address and, when it has a login, must authenticate.

Patterns. INN wildmat lists, comma separated, read left to right, the last match wins: * every group, !comp.* excludes a subtree, @alt.binaries.* poisons the whole article (one matching group refuses the article even when another group of it is wanted). In patterns are the groups the peer may send us, out patterns the groups we offer to the peer. The preset menu under each field fills in a common list, including the newsdeef.eu defaults that pugleaf.net's INN servers use (newsdeef peers also limit the size with the out flag <{{.NewsdeefMaxSize}}).

newsdeef.eu pattern lists (INN newsfeeds variables) 
{{.NewsdeefINN}}

Path token. The peer's own Path identity, the leftmost element it writes into Path:. An article arriving from the peer with that token on the left gets our identity prepended as usual; a different leftmost element is recorded as <token>.MISMATCH. The token also suppresses the outgoing feed: an article whose Path already carries the peer's token is not offered to it again.
