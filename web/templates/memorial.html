In memory of Retro Guy

go-pugleaf carries forward the legacy of Retro Guy of novabbs.com, the visionary lead developer of rslight, the RockSolid Light web gateway to Usenet.

He sadly cannot see this evolution of his work. Rest in peace.

The first go-pugleaf node went online on July 15, 2025, taking over the rocksolid.* hierarchy and the communities he built around it. We are committed to keeping the rocksolid.* community alive and thriving, and this fresh interface and Go-based architecture are our way of honoring his contributions while building for the future.

Built with ❤️ by RockSolid fans.
